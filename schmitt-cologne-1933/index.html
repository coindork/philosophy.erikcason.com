Carl Schmitt · a lecture at the University of Cologne · 16 June 1933

The lecture in eleven parts

About this film

The recording of this lecture and its transcript come from the Carl-Schmitt-Gesellschaft. The film gives the whole lecture as it survives, in English, in Schmitt’s own words, translated line by line from the transcript. One side of the original records is lost; the closing passage begins where the surviving record resumes.

It is a reconstruction, made with AI. Schmitt’s voice is a clone made from the 1933 recording, reading the English, and his face is animated from photographs of him; the film says on screen that both are generated. The archive film, photographs and paintings were chosen for what each line names.
